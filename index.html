A Little About Me

Michael Davis, LMHC

Licensed Mental Health Counselor

I believe meaningful change begins with feeling heard, understood, and accepted. Reaching out for support can take courage, and you deserve a space where you can show up as yourself.

My approach to counseling blends practical, skills-based strategies with conversation, exploration, and self-discovery. Every person is different, and I believe therapy should reflect the individual rather than follow a one-size-fits-all approach.

I work with adults and teenagers, with a particular focus on supporting individuals with autism and ADHD, as well as those navigating anxiety, emotional regulation, and anger management.

Whether you're looking for practical tools to manage everyday challenges or a space to better understand yourself, our work will be guided by your needs, strengths, and goals.
